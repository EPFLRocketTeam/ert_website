03 · THE IT TEAM

Two poles. One IT team.

We want both a reliable foundation and the freedom to build what comes next.

Operations

Keep today's systems reliable.
  • Infrastructure & servers
  • Accounts, access & permissions
  • Wiki & core services
  • Security, backups & reliability
  • IT support
  • Keeping the organisation running

Research & Development

Build systems that make ERT better.
  • Automation & workflows
  • n8n & service integrations
  • Internal tools & data systems
  • AI / LLM / knowledge systems
  • Experimentation with new technology
  • Turn recurring problems into systems
Operations gives R&D a stable platform. R&D reduces the amount of repetitive work Operations has to do. The two poles should continuously make each other better.
04 · WHY IT MATTERS

A lot of low-hanging fruit.

Many opportunities where a motivated IT team can make a visible difference.

📚 Documentation
Important knowledge isn't always documented consistently, making handovers and onboarding harder.
👥 Capacity
IT was understaffed in previous years, so technical debt accumulated.
🧩 Complexity
Non-IT members shouldn't need to understand our entire infrastructure to get things done.
🗂️ Scattered information
Wiki, Drive, Slack, Git repositories, spreadsheets and individual knowledge all coexist.
🤖 Manual repetition
The same data and administrative steps are often repeated by hand instead of being automated.
🧠 Hard-to-use knowledge
ERT has accumulated valuable technical knowledge that is difficult for new members to discover.
